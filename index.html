About me

A little about me

Salam, I'm Mehander. I got into web design because I like making things that people actually use — a shop page that brings orders, a portfolio that gets you a call back, a landing page that explains your work in plain words.

My way of working is simple. First I listen — what do you need, who is it for, what should a visitor do after landing on the page? Then I build it in small steps and show you as I go, so there are no surprises at the end.

I keep my prices and timelines clear from day one, and I test everything on a real phone before I call it done — because most of your visitors will see it on a phone first.
