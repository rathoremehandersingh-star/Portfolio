A little about me

Salam, I'm Mehander Singh — a web designer and developer from Pakistan. I'm still early in my journey: HTML is my strong side, CSS is getting better day by day, and JavaScript is next on my list.

I make simple websites — portfolios, business pages and landing pages. Nothing fancy for the sake of it, just clean pages that are easy to read and work well on a phone, because that's where most people will see them first.
